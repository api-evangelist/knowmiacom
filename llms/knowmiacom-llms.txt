# Knowmia.com

> Knowmia was an education-focused video platform for creating, sharing, and assigning
> instructional video and lessons. It was operated by TechSmith alongside Camtasia and
> Snagit and marketed as Knowmia / Knowmia Pro. TechSmith retired Knowmia Pro in early
> 2025 and directs former customers to TechSmith Screencast. As of July 2026,
> knowmia.com issues an HTTP 301 redirect to the TechSmith Screencast product page.
> Knowmia publishes no public API, developer portal, SDKs, CLI, or machine-readable
> API specifications.
